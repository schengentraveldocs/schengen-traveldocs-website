Common Digital Nomad Visa Documents

Depending on the country and applicant's circumstances, documents may include:

  • Valid passport and application form
  • Proof of remote employment or business activity
  • Evidence of qualifying income
  • Bank statements and financial records
  • Health insurance documentation
  • Police clearance certificate, where required
  • Accommodation or intended residence information
  • Educational or professional documents, where required
  • Certified translations and legalisations, where applicable
Please note: Digital nomad visa requirements differ significantly between countries and may change. Always verify current requirements with the relevant official immigration authority or consulate. A Schengen short-stay visa and a national digital nomad residence route are not the same immigration category.
