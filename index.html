About Schengen TravelDocs

Schengen TravelDocs is an independent visa documentation support initiative focused on helping applicants organise their paperwork and better understand visa application requirements. Our approach is centred on clarity, professional presentation and practical guidance.

Important information: Schengen TravelDocs is an independent documentation assistance service. We are not affiliated with any embassy, consulate, government authority or visa application centre. We do not issue visas, make visa decisions or guarantee approval. Applicants are responsible for checking the latest official requirements and submitting accurate information.
