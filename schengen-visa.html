Commonly Required Schengen Visa Documents

Depending on the applicant's circumstances and destination country, documents may include:

  • Completed visa application form
  • Valid passport and applicable residence documents
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Travel medical insurance
  • Accommodation and travel arrangements
  • Bank statements and financial evidence
  • Employment or business supporting documents
  • Travel purpose and supporting cover letter
  • Travel itinerary and other country-specific documents
Please note: Requirements vary according to nationality, country of application, travel purpose and individual circumstances. Always check the relevant embassy or official visa application centre's latest checklist before applying.
