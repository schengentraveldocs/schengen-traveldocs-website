Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Schengen TravelDocs respects the privacy of visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you contact us or request our services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service you request, we may receive information such as:

  • Name and contact details, including email address and telephone number.
  • Passport and travel information relevant to your enquiry.
  • Employment, financial and accommodation information that you choose to provide for document preparation.
  • Information about your intended travel, visa category and destination.
  • Messages and other information you send to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • Prepare requested checklists, cover letters and travel itineraries.
  • Organise and review supporting documents as part of the requested service.
  • Provide general visa application preparation information.
  • Maintain service records and address customer queries.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may use third-party communication or service providers when necessary to operate our services. Information will only be shared with other parties where necessary for the requested service, where you have authorised it, or where disclosure is required by law.

Unless you specifically authorise us, we do not submit visa applications or send your documents to embassies, consulates or visa application centres on your behalf.

4. Handling of Personal Documents

Please share only the documents and information reasonably necessary for the service you request. Passport copies, bank statements and other personal documents should be treated as confidential.

We aim to limit access to customer information to people who need it to provide the requested service. However, electronic communication and online storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, respond to enquiries, maintain appropriate business records or meet applicable legal requirements. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted or destroyed where reasonably practicable.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate record-keeping obligations.

7. Cookies and Website Information

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and other third-party services may process technical information, such as usage or device information, in accordance with their own policies. We do not currently operate a custom analytics or advertising system on this website.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to official visa information websites and other external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage visitors to review those policies before sharing personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
